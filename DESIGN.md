# DESIGN.md — InvoiceFlow landing

## Visual world (niche: ledger desk)
Ledger green (`#14532d` family) and brass (`#b45309`) on warm paper
(`#faf8f1` family). Serif display, sans body, tabular numerals for money.
Brass rules, ledger hairlines, stamp badges. Dark green bands (hero, final
CTA) with brass mesh glow; light paper content between.

## Landing composition
1. Brass rule above a sticky top bar (64px) with ledger wordmark.
2. Dark ledger hero: serif headline, brass rule, 20-word subtext, primary
   plus demo CTAs. Right: 3D-tilting bookkeeper photography collage with
   shine sweep and functional caption.
3. Glance band: three brass-dot facts (privacy, honest unknowns, fictional
   demo data).
4. Features bento (6 cells): wide scan-photo cell, standard cells, wide
   export cell with photo.
5. Pipeline as numbered hairline rows (distinct from bento), plus an honest
   inputs line.
6. FAQ accordion, dark final CTA, ledger footer.

## Motion (one authored moment)
Pointer-driven 3D tilt on the hero collage, brass orb drift, shine sweep,
floating overlap photo. Scroll reveal elsewhere. All gated by
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Hard rules honored
Zero em/en dashes. No kickers or eyebrows. No fake ledger UI, no invented
vendor names, no filled-track confidence bars, no stamps overlaid on images.
Content-matched photography (ledger desk, payment, documents, laptop export).
